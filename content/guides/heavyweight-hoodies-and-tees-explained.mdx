Fabric weight is how much one square yard (oz/yd²) or one square meter (g/m², also written gsm) of the fabric weighs, so a bigger number means a denser, heavier fabric. At Hustler Dior, the only product with a Printful-stated 500 g/m² weight is the Dog Gone sculpted heavyweight hoodie at 15 oz./yd.² (500 g/m²), and the tees below are stated between 6 oz./yd.² (170 g/m²) and 7.5 oz./yd.² (254 g/m²).

## What fabric weight means

Read the number, not the label. Wear your own rules.

- **oz/yd²** is ounces per square yard, the US unit. **g/m²** is grams per square meter, usually written gsm. It's the same idea in two units.
- **Converting:** 1 oz/yd² is roughly 33.9 g/m². Printful rounds its printed figures, so this guide always uses the oz and g/m² pair exactly as Printful prints it for each product.
- **A bigger number means more fabric per square unit.** That is all the number says. It doesn't rate softness, shrink, durability, warmth or quality.
- **Weight is not fit.** A heavy fabric can be cut slim, and a light one can be cut boxy. For cut, see the [fit guide](https://hustlerdior.com/fit-guide).
- **There's no single cutoff for "heavyweight."** Printful's blank descriptions don't set one, so read the stated number.

## "Heavyweight" is a label. Read the number.

Printful describes blanks as "heavyweight" at 6 oz./yd.² (170 g/m²) (Bella+Canvas 3010), 7.5 oz./yd.² (254 g/m²) (Shaka Wear SHGDD), 10 oz./yd.² (339 g/m²) (Bella+Canvas 4711), and 15 oz./yd.² (500 g/m²) (Stanley/Stella SASU057 / STSU278). One word covers a wide range, so the label alone doesn't tell you which weight you're getting.

## FAQ

### What does gsm mean on a hoodie or t-shirt?

gsm means grams per square meter (g/m²). It's the weight of one square meter of the fabric, so a higher gsm means a denser, heavier fabric. In the US the same idea is written as ounces per square yard (oz/yd²), and 1 oz/yd² is roughly 33.9 g/m². It describes weight only, not softness, warmth or fit.

### How many oz is 500 gsm?

Printful states 15 oz./yd.² (500 g/m²) for the Stanley/Stella Sculpted Heavyweight Hoodie used on the Hustler Dior Dog Gone hoodie. The exact conversion factor is about 33.9 g/m² per oz/yd², and Printful rounds its printed figures, so use the pair as Printful prints it.

### Does Hustler Dior sell a 500 gsm hoodie?

Yes, one. The Hustler Dior Dog Gone Unisex sculpted heavyweight hoodie uses the Stanley/Stella SASU057 / STSU278 blank, which Printful states at 15 oz./yd.² (500 g/m²). No other Hustler Dior hoodie or sweatshirt in this guide is stated at 500 g/m² or higher. The next heaviest are stated at 10 oz./yd.² (339 g/m²).

### Which Hustler Dior tees does Printful describe as boxy?

Printful describes three of the blanks Hustler Dior uses as boxy or dropped-shoulder: Shaka Wear SHGDD at 7.5 oz./yd.² (254 g/m²), Cotton Heritage MC1087 box tee at 7 oz./yd.² (237.34 g/m²), and Bella+Canvas 3010 at 6 oz./yd.² (170 g/m²). Printful states each weight. Browse them in [Tees](https://hustlerdior.com/collections/tees).

### Is a higher gsm always better?

No. gsm only tells you how much a square of fabric weighs. Whether a heavier fabric suits you depends on the cut, the look you want and how you wear it, so check the [fit guide](https://hustlerdior.com/fit-guide) as well as the number.

### Why do different "heavyweight" tees have different weights?

"Heavyweight" is a label, not a measurement. Printful uses it on blanks stated at 6 oz./yd.² (170 g/m²), 7.5 oz./yd.² (254 g/m²), 10 oz./yd.² (339 g/m²) and up to 15 oz./yd.² (500 g/m²), so read the stated oz/yd² or g/m² for the exact piece instead of relying on the word.

### Are Hustler Dior hoodies and tees made in-house?

No. Hustler Dior pieces are made to order through our fulfillment partner Printful, and the weights on this page are the ones Printful states for each blank. See [Help](https://hustlerdior.com/help) for how made-to-order works.
